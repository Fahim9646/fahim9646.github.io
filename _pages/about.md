<div style="text-align: justify;">
  <p>Hello, I am Fahim. I am a civil engineer from the Rajshahi University of Engineering and Technology (RUET), working between experimental soil mechanics and computational modelling. My research interests lie in <b>geotechnical engineering</b>, in particular the <b>discrete element modelling of granular soils</b>, <b>inherent anisotropy</b> and <b>soil-structure interfaces</b>, and in <b>sustainable construction</b> in the context of Bangladesh.</p>
  <p>My undergraduate thesis at RUET, supervised by Prof. Md. Mahmud Sazzad, is a matched experimental and DEM study of how bedding-plane anisotropy carries over from sand to sand-concrete interfaces. I built the DEM engine from scratch and calibrated it against our direct shear tests. The resulting journal manuscript is in preparation, and I am extending the work to sand-pile interfaces under lateral loading.</p>
  <p>I currently work as an Executive, Quality Control (on-site) at Rancon Engineering &amp; Consultants Ltd., with the Project Management Office on site quality, schedules, documentation and reporting. Before that, I was a Planning Engineer in the Civil Engineering Department of Mir Cement Ltd. (Narayanganj Factory), handling project scheduling, cost control and daily quality inspections.</p>
  <p>I plan to continue with graduate research (MSc/PhD) and, in the long run, move into academia. I am happy to hear from prospective supervisors and collaborators.</p>
